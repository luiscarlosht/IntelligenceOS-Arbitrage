# Reviewed catalog selector and report fixes

Stored catalog selection compares explicit brand, model, net contents, count and
variant evidence. Different titles can remain plausible; titles cannot establish
identity. Equal supporting evidence retains alternatives and selects no winner.
Brand and manufacturer, unit counts and retail package quantity remain separate.

Candidate comparison workbooks use a source-neutral title column and retain saved
Walmart item prices. Observed prices never establish verified acquisition costs.
Failed and unattempted outcomes remain visible. Regression coverage includes
word-order variation, ties, conflicts, unknowns and source-price presentation.

This change makes no provider requests, credential reads, emails or Sheet writes.
